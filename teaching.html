UAV and Pix4D Image Processing

UAV photo taken in an undergraduate course

During my time at National Central University in Taiwan, I taught an undergraduate course on UAV and Pix4D image processing as an Instructor. I designed the course to be very hands-on and project-based, with a focus on practical skills and field experiments. The students learned how to operate UAVs, collect aerial imagery, and process the data using Pix4D software to create 3D models and maps. The course was well-received by the students and provided them with valuable skills in geospatial analysis and remote sensing.
